Privacy policy

This policy covers Sketché, an Android drawing app made by Foundy Works (package name works.foundy.sketche). The short version: Sketché collects nothing, and your drawings never leave your device unless you send them somewhere yourself.

  1. 01Information we collect

    None. Sketché does not collect, store on our behalf, sell or share any personal data. There is no account to create and no sign in.

  2. 02No analytics, ads or tracking

    Sketché contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking SDKs. It does not use advertising identifiers.

  3. 03Network access and the support purchase

    Drawing never needs a connection. The app includes Google Play Billing for one optional, one-time purchase that supports the app and unlocks the Dark and System themes. That library adds the Android network permissions, and Google Play uses them only to process the purchase and to check whether you already own it. Sketché never sends your drawings, projects or any other content over the network. The purchase itself is handled by Google Play under Google’s privacy policy; Sketché receives only whether the purchase is owned.

  4. 04Where your work is stored

    Your drawings, projects, brushes, palettes and settings are stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage. This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device to device transfer, so it is not copied to any cloud service by the app.

  5. 05When files leave your device

    Files leave your device only when you explicitly export or share them, using Android’s share sheet or the system file picker (the Storage Access Framework). They go to the destination you choose, and that destination’s own privacy terms then apply.

  6. 06Files and permissions

    Sketché only reads the images and files you pick yourself through the system picker. It does not request access to your camera, location, contacts or microphone.

  7. 07Children’s privacy

    Sketché collects no data from anyone, including children.

  8. 08Deleting your data

    Uninstalling Sketché removes all of its data from your device. Export any projects you want to keep first.

  9. 09This page

    This page sets no cookies, uses no analytics, runs no scripts and makes no requests to third parties.

  10. 10Changes to this policy

    If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

  11. 11Contact

    Questions about this policy can be sent through foundy.works.
